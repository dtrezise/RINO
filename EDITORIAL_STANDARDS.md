# RINO editorial standards — WORKING v0.1

## Scope

RINO may advocate strongly, but factual claims must remain narrower than the proof. The website and app distinguish documented fact, record status, inference, analysis, opinion, proposal, and adopted position.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Controlling law, official filings, court records, legislation, agency guidance, and original public records.
2. Direct statements, datasets, transcripts, and methodologically transparent research.
3. Reputable reporting used as a bridge to primary records or to document what was reported.
4. Commentary used as analysis, never as automatic proof of an underlying fact.

Every source record carries a publisher, title, URL, retrieval date, authority tier, access state, locator, and copyright-handling status. Short excerpts or paraphrases must be proportionate and necessary. Do not copy news articles wholesale.

## Claim handling

- Separate allegation, charge, conviction, civil liability, settlement, dismissal, acquittal, reversal, pardon, reporting, and analysis.
- Do not infer hidden intent without unusually strong disclosed evidence.
- Avoid guilt by relationship.
- Preserve meaningful denials, responses, appeals, uncertainty, and contrary facts.
- Review headlines, status labels, summaries, social copy, and preview metadata independently; each may circulate alone.
- Use `WORKING`, `ILLUSTRATIVE`, or `DAN NEEDED` when a reader could otherwise mistake a proposal for an adopted position or established status.

## eBox publication gate

Before substantive public release, an eBox needs a unique permanent ID and slug; claim register; adequate sources; explicit source relationships; limiting context; separate analysis; tests only where applicable; review status and date; canonical route; social copy; correction path; and private expert brief where expert review would materially improve accuracy.

## Tests and scores

A test asks a defined question of existing evidence. It does not rewrite the evidence or force a result. Criteria scored `N/A` are excluded from the denominator. Public scores require stored criterion-level rationales and editorial review. Automated classification may suggest relevance but cannot publish a score.

## Corrections and revisions

Material corrections append a dated revision entry identifying the affected claim, prior wording, new wording, reason, and source. Quiet typographic fixes may be made without a material revision entry. A correction must propagate to the headline, summary, social copy, structured export, and app if those surfaces are affected.
