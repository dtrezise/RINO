# Expert interview pipeline — private companion design

Every substantive eBox should receive a private companion brief before expert outreach. The public eBox exposes only whether a companion exists and its general review state.

## Private fields

Permanent eBox ID; expertise domains; at least two independently verified candidates; public professional profile routes; candidate-specific fit; interview angle; disconfirming questions; conflicts and disclosure notes; review date; outreach state; consent state; recording and quotation permissions; publication decision.

Do not store home addresses, private phone numbers, private personal email addresses, family information, or irrelevant personal data. Do not populate candidates merely to make the pipeline appear active.

## Workflow

1. Define the expertise needed from the claim and its contrary possibilities.
2. Verify professional standing from primary institutional profiles and independent work.
3. Prepare candidate-specific questions that could weaken or change the working claim.
4. Review conflicts, political activity relevant to disclosure, and representation language.
5. `DAN NEEDED`: approve candidate, contact, RINO representation, and outreach copy.
6. Obtain consent for contact, recording, quotation, editing, and publication.
7. Preserve meaningful qualification and disagreement in the final public companion.
8. Link material corrections back to the eBox revision history.
