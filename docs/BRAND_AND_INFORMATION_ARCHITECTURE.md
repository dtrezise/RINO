# RINO brand and information architecture — WORKING

## Identity idea

The first-pass identity treats RINO as an editorial seal, not a cartoon mascot. A circular `R` seal, disciplined four-letter grid, registration marks, status stamps, paper rules, and evidence-ledger typography signal an institution under construction. No government seal, flag lockup, official eagle, or misleading public-authority device is used.

## Voice

- Direct without swagger.
- Serious without bureaucratic impersonation.
- Optimistic without inventing momentum.
- Patriotic without policing heritage or belonging.
- Capable of saying “not established,” “we do not know,” and “this is a working question.”

System sans-serif is used for navigation and body copy; an editorial old-style serif is used for display hierarchy. Motion is restrained and removed when the user prefers reduced motion.

## Information architecture

Home provides the proposition, status disclosure, working principles, evidence entry points, acronym lab, and participation gate. Principles defines civic posture. Platform holds questions and process rather than invented positions. Evidence is the permanent record archive. Act stages contribution routes without collecting data. Voices accurately displays an empty editorial pipeline. Methods publishes the evidence and scoring contract. Supporting status, corrections, privacy, and terms routes preserve accountability without crowding primary navigation.
